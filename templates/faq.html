General Information

1- When and where is the festival?

-The festival is on 20th of June 2024, at Wembley Stadium, HA9 0WS, from 12:00 PM to 11:00 PM.

2- Is the festival all-ages?

-Yes, but attendees under 18 must be accompanied by an adult.

Tickets

3- How can I buy tickets?

-Tickets are available on our official website and through authorized ticketing partners.

4- Can I get a refund?

-No, all ticket sales are final. However, you can transfer your ticket to someone else.

Entry and Security

5- What should I bring?

-Bring your ticket (printed or digital), a valid ID, sunscreen, a hat, and a reusable water bottle.

6- Will there be security checks?

-Yes, all attendees will undergo bag checks and security screenings.

Accommodation and Travel

7- Is there parking?

-Yes, parking is available at Wembley Stadium. We recommend purchasing parking passes online.

8- How can I get to the festival by public transport?

-Wembley Stadium is accessible by buses and trains.

On-Site Services

9- Will food and drinks be available?

-Yes, a variety of food trucks and beverage stands will be present.

10- Are there ATMs?

-Yes, ATMs will be available on-site.

11- What if I lose something?

-Visit the lost and found near the main entrance or contact us via our website.

Health and Safety

12- What health measures are in place?

-We have medical staff, hand sanitizing stations, and enhanced cleaning protocols.

13- Where is the first aid station?

-First aid stations are located throughout the grounds.

14- What should I do in an emergency?

-Locate the nearest security personnel. Emergency exits are clearly marked.

Additional Tips

-Stay Hydrated: Drink plenty of water.

-Dress Comfortably: Wear comfortable clothing and shoes.

-Plan Ahead: Familiarize yourself with the festival map.
